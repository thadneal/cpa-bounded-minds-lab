# parameterized-falsification-v1 Results

Status: **consumed exploratory falsification evidence**

Run completed under v0.10.0 on 2026-08-20.

The phase executed six registered 7x7 response surfaces with seven deterministic replicates per cell:

```text
294 controlled cells
2,058 deterministic runs
176 / 294 cells with negative mean primary margin
1,201 / 2,058 replicates crossing at least one registered primary boundary
```

This is not a validation pass rate. The probes deliberately intervene outside the original frozen world-generator support. Negative regions are useful evidence about the operating envelope of the local mechanism copied into each micro-assay.

## Protocol 03 - developmental-history informativeness

Surface: history instability x present rule error.

```text
negative mean cells          40 / 49
negative-replicate cells     44 / 49
replicate margin range       -0.065215 to 0.008267
```

The frozen developmental-standing calculation increasingly discounts a source as its history becomes unstable. The controlled surface shows that this can become net harmful when the source's final rule is presently useful. Instability is therefore not intrinsically negative evidence. It may indicate unreliable development, but it may also record successful adaptation to a changing world.

Carry forward the narrower pressure:

> Developmental history should change the *kind and shape of uncertainty* attached to a transferred conclusion. It should not automatically become a scalar penalty for having changed.

The particular P03 standing equation should remain an experimental instrument rather than CPA anatomy.

## Protocol 04 - equal-budget comparator

Surface: warrant asymmetry x minority-correct fraction.

```text
negative mean cells          13 / 49
negative-replicate cells     13 / 49
replicate margin range       -0.561163 to 0.598526
```

Both paths received identical estimate, standing, uncertainty information, and communication cost. The frozen typed weighted reducer competed against a standing-weighted median rather than the original early-smoothing control.

No aggregation rule dominated the whole surface. Robust consensus was better when the two-report majority was reliably correct. The weighted reducer became useful as minority correctness rose because it preserved minority influence rather than selecting the majority outright. At extreme warrant asymmetry, the weighted median could again dominate when the highly warranted minority was almost always correct.

Carry forward:

> Preserve epistemic shape through the communication boundary, but do not prescribe one universal reducer merely because it won against a weaker control.

Protocol 04's most durable finding concerns the public representation of warrant, not its original weighted aggregation rule.

## Protocol 05 - convention volatility

Surface: repeated regime-change frequency x change magnitude.

```text
negative mean cells          40 / 49
negative-replicate cells     42 / 49
replicate margin range       -0.117211 to 0.004148
```

The most informative failure was not simply "more change is worse." Small or moderate changes could be especially dangerous because a stale convention remained useful enough to clear the frozen success threshold and therefore reinforced itself. Larger changes sometimes produced stronger corrective pressure and triggered revision sooner.

At very high change frequency the mechanism often fell back toward repeated negotiation. Utility could recover while the communication advantage disappeared. That is a form of graceful degradation rather than successful cultural compression.

Carry forward:

> A convention must remain sensitive to degrading fit before failure becomes dramatic. Revisability and stability are opposing pressures, and "good enough" performance can create cultural hysteresis.

The exact frozen success threshold and convention-standing rule should not be treated as architecture.

## Protocol 06 - ancestry opacity

Surface: origin-hint missingness x developmental-signature separation.

```text
negative mean cells          28 / 49
negative-replicate cells     28 / 49
replicate margin range       -0.046795 to 0.010940
```

Origin-hint missingness had surprisingly little effect. Signature separation dominated the outcome. In the controlled assay, independent roots with developmental-signature separation of 0.05, 0.10, or 0.15 were effectively always merged, while separation of 0.30 or more eliminated the measured false-merge problem across the surface.

The reason is structural: the frozen heuristic can merge reports when either origin hints match **or** signatures are sufficiently close. A known different origin does not act as negative evidence against a signature-based merge.

Carry forward:

> Provenance must provide evidence both *for* shared dependence and *against* shared dependence. Epistemic ancestry should be represented as uncertain dependence, not categorical identity or hard deduplication.

The P06 grouping heuristic is therefore falsified as a candidate durable implementation despite the useful mechanism-level finding that dependence-aware corroboration can suppress echo amplification.

## Protocol 07 - recommender reliability, mismatch prevalence, and severity

Two independent surfaces were required because challenge-v1 had mixed distinct failure modes.

### Reliability x mismatch prevalence

```text
negative mean cells          29 / 49
negative-replicate cells     30 / 49
replicate margin range       -0.074044 to 0.009686
```

The cleanest component is opportunity cost. As recommender credibility rises, useful recommendations can matter sooner, but widespread local mismatch also becomes more expensive before consequence corrects the receiver.

Some negative combined margins at zero or very low mismatch are not meaningful failures of provisional standing. In a world where every recommendation generalizes, inherited authority may legitimately outperform a deliberately capped provisional path. The combined primary criterion is therefore not a universal interpretation surface.

Carry forward:

> Recommendation credibility and expected *generalizability to this receiver and context* are different variables. Credibility alone cannot determine transferred opportunity.

### Reliability x strong mismatch severity

```text
negative mean cells          26 / 49
negative-replicate cells     29 / 49
replicate margin range       -0.137751 to 0.008843
```

Residual standing follows contradiction magnitude continuously. The old categorical "strong mismatch" label hid that relationship. Sufficiently large contradiction eventually drives standing down, while milder but persistent mismatch can remain too influential. At high credibility and high mismatch severity, eventual correction can still arrive only after unacceptable developmental cost has already been paid.

Carry forward:

> Revocation should respond proportionally to contradiction strength and consequence history. Eventual correction does not erase the cost incurred before correction.

## Phase-level conclusion

parameterized-falsification-v1 materially increases confidence in the laboratory as an instrument because it willingly made frozen mechanisms fail in interpretable ways.

The progression is now:

```text
mechanism discovery
    -> frozen unseen histories
    -> adversarial generator search
    -> controlled causal intervention
```

The durable output is not a set of constants. It is a set of behavioral pressures and failure warnings:

- developmental instability is information, not an automatic trust penalty;
- public epistemic shape should survive communication without forcing one universal aggregation rule;
- convention can become self-reinforcing while merely "good enough" under subtle drift;
- ancestry inference must preserve negative provenance evidence and uncertainty about dependence;
- social standing transfer needs an estimate of local generalizability as well as recommender credibility;
- authority repair should be proportional and its path-dependent cost remains part of the outcome.

The six surfaces are now consumed exploratory evidence. They may be reproduced, but tuning against them requires a later fresh validation design.

## Consequence for the next protocol

The falsification phase also makes a remaining social assumption more visible: Protocols 01-07 treat public evidence as noisy, incomplete, correlated, or locally mismatched, but not as a surface that another mind deliberately learns to optimize for influence.

Protocol 08 therefore asks whether consequence can keep strategic public influence answerable **without requiring access to the sender's private interior**.
